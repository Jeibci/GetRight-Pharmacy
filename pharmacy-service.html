The Impact of Pharmacy Services : on Healthcare Delivery

Published: May 2026

Pharmacy services : go far beyond dispensing medicines. Pharmacists today play a critical role in medication counseling, chronic disease management, preventive healthcare, immunization support, and patient education.

Accessible pharmacy services : help reduce hospital congestion, improve medication adherence, and support better health outcomes.

As healthcare systems continue to evolve, community pharmacies are becoming one of the most accessible healthcare touchpoints for millions of people.

GetRight Pharmacy : stands out in the Surulere axis providing proficient pharmacy services for over 1000 inhabitants throughout 2005. Some of these services include Blood pressure services, Blood Glucose checks, BMI checks, Hepatitis tests and many more.

Investing in pharmacy services is investing in stronger healthcare delivery.
