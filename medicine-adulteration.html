Medicine Adulteration : The Dangerous Threat Hiding in Plain Sight

Published: May 2026

Medicine adulteration remains a major threat to patient safety in Nigeria and many developing countries. Fake, substandard, and improperly stored medicines continue to circulate within the healthcare system, putting millions of lives at risk.

Adulterated medicines can lead to treatment failure, antimicrobial resistance, organ damage, and even death.

Patients must be encouraged to purchase medicines only from licensed pharmacies and accredited healthcare facilities. Regulatory agencies, pharmaceutical companies, healthcare providers, and the public must work together to eliminate counterfeit medicines from circulation.
